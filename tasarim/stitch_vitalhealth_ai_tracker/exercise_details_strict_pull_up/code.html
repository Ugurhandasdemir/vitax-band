Execution

1

The Setup

Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width using an overhand grip (palms facing away). Hang freely with arms fully extended.

2

The Pull

Engage your core and pull your chest toward the bar by driving your elbows down and back. Squeeze your shoulder blades together.

3

The Descent

Lower yourself under control back to the starting position until your arms are fully extended. Do not drop rapidly.
